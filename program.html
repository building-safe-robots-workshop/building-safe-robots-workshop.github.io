Proposed Workshop Program

A full-day format is proposed for ICRA 2027. The detailed schedule, speakers and timings are to be announced, subject to workshop acceptance.

Three application domains

  1. Safety in Healthcare, Assistive, and Surgical Robotics
  2. Safety in Industry and Manufacturing
  3. Safety Beyond Structured Workspaces: Field and High-Consequence Robotics

Within each domain

  • Academic perspective: research capabilities and methods considered close to deployment, including their assumptions, limitations and open questions.
  • Practitioner perspective: existing solutions, concrete use cases, deployment experience and remaining technical or organisational barriers.
  • Safety Translation Debate: a moderated discussion with both speakers and a certification or regulation expert, connecting research claims to validation, standards and assurance evidence.

Contributed work and interactive clinics

Selected contributed papers are planned for oral presentations. Authors will be invited to formulate a concrete question about translation, deployment, safety, validation or certification in advance. These questions will feed into the domain discussions and interactive clinics.

Cross-domain synthesis

The workshop will close with an organizer-led synthesis with certification and regulation contributors. The resulting Lab-to-Real Safety Gap Map will capture recurring barriers, domain-specific differences, common knowledge and skills, and priority research and validation gaps.
