Workshop Program

Three application domains. Research, practice and certification in conversation.

Each 90-minute domain session combines an academic presentation (25 minutes), a practitioner presentation (25 minutes), a certification and validation perspective (15 minutes), and a moderated Safety Translation Debate (25 minutes). Participant questions and contributed work inform the discussions.

Date: TBD Time: 08:30–18:00 (tentative) Format: Full day
