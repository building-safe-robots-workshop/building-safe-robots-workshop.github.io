Call for Papers

The call for papers is not yet open. Submission instructions, deadlines and presentation details will be announced once the workshop plans are confirmed.

The proposed workshop connects robot safety and embodied AI research with deployment, validation, assurance and certification across healthcare, manufacturing and field robotics.

Contributed work is intended to engage directly with the domain discussions through concrete questions about the transition from research to real-world use.
