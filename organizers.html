Industrial Support and Endorsement

NOKOV Motion Capture is an industrial collaborator endorsing the 2nd Workshop on Building Safe Robots.

NOKOV provides high-precision optical motion capture systems and has empowered 1,500+ robotics labs worldwide, supporting research in areas such as humanoids, mobile robots, manipulation, multi-robot systems, and more.

Manufacturing Technology Centre

The Manufacturing Technology Centre (MTC) supports the workshop’s industrial engagement, bringing expertise in industrial robotics, technology transfer and real-world deployment.

MTC is a UK manufacturing and technology organisation that combines engineering expertise with advanced technologies to help businesses develop products, improve processes and adopt automation. Its work in robotics and manufacturing connects the workshop’s research discussions with practical safety, validation and deployment challenges.

Technical Committee Support

The workshop is endorsed by the IEEE RAS Technical Committee on Human–Robot Interaction.

The committee brings together research on robots that share spaces and tasks with people, including physical safety, human factors and human-aware planning and control. Its support strengthens the workshop’s connection with the international human–robot interaction community.

The workshop is endorsed by the IEEE RAS Technical Committee on Robotics for Nuclear Environments.

The committee connects researchers and practitioners developing robotics for nuclear inspection, maintenance, decommissioning and incident response. Its support strengthens the workshop’s connection with the nuclear robotics community and its focus on safe deployment in hazardous environments.
