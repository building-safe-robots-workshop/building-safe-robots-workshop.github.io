Building Safe Robots — Beyond the Lab

From Robotics and Embodied AI Research to Deployable Systems

Proposed ICRA 2027 workshop. The full proposal is in preparation. Acceptance, date and venue are not yet confirmed.

Second edition · Full day

Connecting research capabilities with the operational requirements, validation evidence and certification pathways needed for responsible deployment.

Robotic systems are advancing rapidly in perception, learning, planning and control. Deploying a complete system requires safety to span robot design, autonomy, human interaction, validation and certification, with assumptions and evidence that remain meaningful in real operating conditions.

Following the first Building Safe Robots workshop at IROS 2025, this second edition brings together academic, practitioner and certification perspectives across three application domains:

  • Safety in Healthcare, Assistive, and Surgical Robotics: close interaction with people and vulnerable users.
  • Safety in Industry and Manufacturing: deployment under productivity, human interaction and operational constraints.
  • Safety Beyond Structured Workspaces: Field and High-Consequence Robotics: changing environments and demanding operating conditions.

From research claims to deployment evidence

Academic speakers will discuss capabilities they consider ready, or close to ready, for real-world use. Practitioners will examine those claims through concrete applications and deployment experience. Certification and regulation experts will discuss the evidence, validation and assurance needed to connect the two.

Moderated Safety Translation Debates, interactive clinics and contributed presentations will bring participants into the discussion. A closing synthesis will produce a Lab-to-Real Safety Gap Map of shared barriers, domain-specific challenges, and priority research and assurance questions.
